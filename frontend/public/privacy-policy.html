Gym360 Privacy Policy

Effective date: From Jan 2026

1. Who we are

Gym360 (we, us, our) operates the Gym360 website and member system, which lets members view sessions, make bookings, and take part in the gym community. This policy explains how we handle the personal information of our members.

2. Information we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address and password (passwords are stored in a secured, hashed form, never as plain text).
  • Role and profile information: whether you are a member, trainer or administrator, and any profile details you choose to add.
  • Booking and session activity: the sessions you book, cancel or attend, and their locations and times.
  • Content you post: posts or messages you submit through the site.
  • Technical information: an authentication key saved in your browser so you stay logged in, and basic server logs such as IP address, browser type and time of access.

3. How we use your information

  • To create and manage your member account and log you in securely.
  • To manage bookings, sessions, trainers and locations.
  • To contact you about your account, bookings, or changes to our services.
  • To keep the site safe, prevent misuse and fix technical problems.
  • To meet our legal obligations.

4. Browser storage and cookies

When you log in, we save an authentication key in your browser local storage. It lets the site recognise you when you return and is removed when you log out. We do not use it for advertising or tracking across other websites.

5. Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information. We only share it with:

  • Our staff and trainers, to the extent needed to run sessions and bookings.
  • Service providers who host or support our systems (for example our web hosting and database provider), who may only use it to provide those services.
  • Authorities or other parties where the law requires or permits it.

6. Storage and security

Your information is stored in a password-protected database on our hosting provider servers. We take reasonable steps to protect it from misuse, loss and unauthorised access, including restricting access to staff who need it. No online system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. How long we keep your information

We keep your information while your membership is active and for a reasonable period afterwards for record-keeping and legal purposes. When it is no longer needed, we delete it or make it anonymous.

8. Accessing and correcting your information

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete your account by contacting us at Gym360. We may need to confirm your identity first, and some information may need to be kept for legal reasons.

9. Children

Our services are intended for adults. If a member is under 18, we expect a parent or guardian to manage the account or give consent.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page, with the effective date shown above.

11. Contact us and complaints

If you have questions or a privacy concern, contact us at Gym360
